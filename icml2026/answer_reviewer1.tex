\subsection*{Weakness 1: Limited empirical validation}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

We have extended the Monte Carlo validation in two directions,
updating both figures.

\paragraph{Updated Figure~1: full $(\alpha, T)$ phase diagrams.}
We replace the theory-only Figure~1 with side-by-side theory
and MC phase maps from comprehensive GPU-accelerated
Metropolis--Hastings simulations covering
$\alpha \in [0.01, 0.55]$ (step $0.01$) and
$T \in [0.025, 2.0]$ (step $0.05$).
We use an adaptive-$N$ scheme
$N(\alpha) = \lfloor \ln M(\alpha)/\alpha \rfloor$,
giving $N \in [24, 990]$ and
$M \in [20{,}000,\, 500{,}000]$ patterns across the full
$\alpha$ range, with 64--512 independent trials per
$(\alpha, T)$ point.
The maps confirm all central predictions: $\alpha_c(0)=0.5$
for both kernels; qualitatively different finite-temperature
behavior of LSE vs.\ LSR; and the sharp threshold
$\alpha_{\mathrm{th}} = (1-b^{-1})^2/2 \approx 0.25$ for
LSR below which retrieval is perfect at any temperature.

\paragraph{Updated Figure~2: alignment curves with error bars.}
We replace the original single-run MC with a multi-trial
protocol. Each of the \textbf{50 independent trials} per
$(\alpha, T)$ point uses a freshly sampled pattern set and
an independent Markov chain, run for 8{,}192 equilibration
+ 4{,}096 sampling steps with step size
$\sigma = 2.4T/\!\sqrt{N}$ and
$\varphi_{\mathrm{init}} \sim \mathcal{U}(0.75, 1.0)$.
Error bars show $\mathrm{SEM} = \hat{\sigma}/\!\sqrt{N_{\mathrm{tr}}}$.

Results are shown at $\alpha=0.05$, $N=100$ and
$\alpha=0.1$, $N=50$. Both give
$p = e^{\alpha N} \approx 148$ patterns, probing the theory
at two different $(\alpha, N)$ points while holding $p$
fixed. For LSR, both $\alpha$ values fall below
$\alpha_{\mathrm{th}}=0.25$, so the two curves overlap
within error bars, directly confirming the predicted
$\alpha$-independence of retrieval below threshold.

We will add the following clarifications to the revision:

geometric entropy is kernel-independent because the
configuration-space volume $\Omega$ in Eq.~(47) depends

and is therefore identical for any $O(N)$ energy depending
on~$x$ only through~$\varphi$;

of $q=\varphi^2$ as isotropic thermal fluctuations
perpendicular to the target pattern;
(3)~at the opening of Section~3, brief inline definitions of
$q_{ab}$, the $n\to 0$ limit, and replica symmetry for
readers unfamiliar with the statistical-mechanics formalism.
